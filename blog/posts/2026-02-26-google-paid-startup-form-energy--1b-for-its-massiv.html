The deal was finalized this week to ensure Google’s data centers never go dark, regardless of weather or grid instability.

This massive cash injection paves the way for Form Energy to launch a new funding round. Insiders expect the company to go public as early as next year. While competitors are struggling with 4-hour lithium-ion limits, Google is now sitting on a 100-hour lead.

The Death of the 4-Hour Battery

Most businesses are still betting on lithium-ion batteries that die after a few hours of use. It’s a losing game. If the sun doesn’t shine for two days, your operations are dead in the water.

  • 100-Hour Storage: Iron-air batteries breathe in oxygen to convert iron to rust, then back again, storing energy for days.
  • Fractional Cost: Using iron instead of lithium or cobalt makes long-duration storage 10x cheaper.
  • Grid Independence: This tech allows massive facilities to operate entirely off-grid during peak pricing or outages.
